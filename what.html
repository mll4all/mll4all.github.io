The method: dissect, don't memorize

Our root-scaffolding method encourages memorization of the basic building blocks of medical terminology rather than full-on words. Medical terms are made of predictable parts — a prefix, a root, and a suffix — and once you know the parts, you can decode words you've never seen before.

A worked example

nephritis

nephr/o (kidney) + -itis (inflammation)  =  inflammation of the kidney.

A longer one

lymphadenopathy

lymph/o (lymph) + aden/o (gland) + -pathy (disease)  =  disease of the lymph glands.

Our programs

Community Workshops

We partner with community organizations such as libraries, schools, and healthcare organizations to provide workshops that promote public education on understanding medical terminology. Volunteers show patients, community health workers, and students how to use our Medical Decoder Guide.

Learning Resources

We are developing a comprehensive online resource hub on our website, which will make understanding health literacy more accessible to a wider audience. We currently have our Medical Decoder Guide and various digital resources, which are constantly adapting as we adjust our curriculum.

Flashcards & Games

Our interactive, digital version of the Medical Decoder Guide. Flip through root cards at your own pace, then test yourself in quiz mode — filtered by prefix, suffix, or root. Active recall through play is consistently shown to beat passive rereading for long-term memory.
