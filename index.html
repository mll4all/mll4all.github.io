Health Outreach · Rooted in the Classics

Take medical words apart,
one root at a time.

Long medical terms aren't memorized — they're dissected. We teach people to break complex terminology into its ancient Greek and Latin building blocks, so a word like tachycardia becomes simply tachy- (fast) + cardi/o (heart).
